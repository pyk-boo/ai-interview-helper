A free, open-source Interview Coder alternative

Screenshot a coding task, get working code with an explanation. AI Interview Helper also listens to spoken questions, supports 7 AI providers and costs nothing.

Download free for Windows Source on GitHub

Updated October 2026

Interview Coder is a paid desktop app focused on coding interviews. You capture the problem on screen and it generates a solution. Third-party reviews describe it as one of the more expensive tools in this category.

AI Interview Helper covers the same screenshot-to-solution workflow for free, and adds voice mode for the spoken parts of an interview: behavioral questions, system design and follow-ups.

Why people look for an Interview Coder alternative

  • Price. Hundreds of dollars is a lot for a tool you need for a few weeks of job hunting.
  • More than coding. Most interview loops also include behavioral and system design rounds, where voice mode helps.
  • Model choice. Strong coding models change fast. With your own key you can switch to the newest one the day it ships.

What you get with AI Interview Helper

  • Voice → answer. Whisper transcribes the interviewer in real time, and the answer appears as soon as they stop talking.
  • Screenshot → solution. Ctrl+Shift+Space sends your screen to a vision model for coding tasks, SQL, diagrams and quizzes.
  • Speaker separation. System audio is treated as the interviewer and your mic as you, so it only answers the other side.
  • 7 AI providers. Groq, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT, DeepSeek, Z.ai GLM, or a local model with Ollama. If one hits a rate limit, the request moves to the next.
  • Personal answers. Paste your resume and target role, and answers come in first person, based on your experience.
  • Any language. It replies in the language of the question: English, Spanish, Russian, German, French and many more.
  • Private by design. No account, no backend. Your API keys stay on your computer.

Tips for coding tasks

  • For hard algorithm problems, pick a strong model such as Claude Opus or GPT-OSS 120B.
  • For screenshots, the model must support images: Gemini Flash, Claude and GPT-4o all do.
  • Ask follow-ups in the chat ("explain the complexity", "rewrite in Java") to understand the solution, not just copy it.

When Interview Coder might be the better choice

If you want a product built only around coding problems, with the vendor managing models and support, a paid tool like Interview Coder may suit you better.

Honest limitations

  • You need your own AI key. Free tiers from Gemini, Groq and Z.ai are enough for most people, and Ollama needs no key at all.
  • There is a ready installer for Windows only. On macOS you run it from source with Node.js.
  • There is no mobile app and no hosted cloud version.
  • The Windows app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning on first launch.

How to switch in 3 steps

  1. Download the Windows installer (or run it from source on macOS).
  2. Create a free Gemini key at aistudio.google.com and paste it into the app settings. One key covers chat, voice and screenshots.
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+L to listen or Ctrl+Shift+Space to solve what's on screen. The full setup guide covers audio and model choice.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Interview Coder?

Yes. AI Interview Helper is free and open source. Press Ctrl+Shift+Space to send a coding task on your screen to a vision model and get working code with an explanation.

Does it work for LeetCode, HackerRank and CodeSignal tasks?

Yes. It works with anything visible on your screen, including LeetCode, HackerRank, CodeSignal and shared editors.

Which programming languages does it support?

Any language the AI model knows: Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, Go, SQL and more. Just ask for the language you need.

Can it help with behavioral and system design questions too?

Yes. Voice mode transcribes spoken questions and suggests answers, using your resume for behavioral questions.
