A free, open-source Final Round AI alternative

Get a real-time AI interview copilot without a subscription. AI Interview Helper listens to questions, reads your screen and suggests answers, with any AI model you choose.

Final Round AI is a paid career platform. Next to its live interview copilot it offers mock interviews, resume tools and other job-search features. Third-party reviews report that live copilot sessions require a paid plan.

If what you need is the live copilot part, AI Interview Helper gives you that for free, as an open-source desktop app.

Why people look for a Final Round AI alternative

  • Price. A monthly plan is expensive if you only need help for a few interviews.
  • Try before you trust. With a free app you can test it in mock interviews as much as you want.
  • Control. Your keys, your model, your data path. Nothing runs through a third-party service.

What you get with AI Interview Helper

  • Voice → answer. Whisper transcribes the interviewer in real time, and the answer appears as soon as they stop talking.
  • Screenshot → solution. Ctrl+Shift+Space sends your screen to a vision model for coding tasks, SQL, diagrams and quizzes.
  • Speaker separation. System audio is treated as the interviewer and your mic as you, so it only answers the other side.
  • 7 AI providers. Groq, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT, DeepSeek, Z.ai GLM, or a local model with Ollama. If one hits a rate limit, the request moves to the next.
  • Personal answers. Paste your resume and target role, and answers come in first person, based on your experience.
  • Any language. It replies in the language of the question: English, Spanish, Russian, German, French and many more.
  • Private by design. No account, no backend. Your API keys stay on your computer.

When Final Round AI might be the better choice

If you want an all-in-one platform with mock interviews, resume building and job-search tools under one account, Final Round AI offers more than a single desktop app.

Honest limitations

  • You need your own AI key. Free tiers from Groq, Gemini and Z.ai are enough for most people, and Ollama needs no key at all.
  • There is a ready installer for Windows only. On macOS you run it from source with Node.js.
  • There is no mobile app and no hosted cloud version.
  • The Windows app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning on first launch.

How to switch in 3 steps

  1. Download the Windows installer (or run it from source on macOS).
  2. Create a free key at console.groq.com and paste it into the app settings.
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+L to listen or Ctrl+Shift+Space to solve what's on screen. The full setup guide covers audio and model choice.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to Final Round AI?

Yes. AI Interview Helper is a free, open-source AI interview copilot with real-time voice transcription and screen analysis, and no subscription.

Can I use it for mock interviews?

Yes. Run a practice interview with a friend or a list of recorded questions, and the app shows how a strong answer could sound.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Download the app, paste a free AI key, and it works. There is no account and no server.

Does it support languages other than English?

Yes. It replies in the language of the question, including Spanish, Russian, German, French and many more.
